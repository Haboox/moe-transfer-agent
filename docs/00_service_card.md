## About this service
This service aims to provide applicants with equivalency certificate for Grades 2-11 issued from general education institutes outside the UAE, in accordance with the standards approved by the Ministry.

## Service Process
1. Log in using UAE Pass
2. Fill out the application and upload the required documents.
3. Pay the applicable fees.
4. Receive the General Education Certificate Equivalency Decision via the email registered in the UAE Pass.

## Required documents
Attach copies of the documents mentioned below:
- Valid passport and
- Valid Emirates ID.
- Valid UAE Visa for non-GCC Nationals.
- Certificates of the previous 2 years of study.
- Legal translation if the certificate was issued in language other than English or Arabic.
- Additional supporting documents (maximum of 2 documents)

Notes:
- Please ensure that the attachments comply with the certificate equivalency requirements stated in the service.
- If there are multiple documents for the same requirement, please merge them into a single file.
- The accepted file format is PDF, and the size limit for each file is 5 MB.

## Service Time
30 working days. (Helpful Information panel: "Up to 30 days")

## Fees
50 AED (Helpful Information panel: "Up to AED 50")

## Service Conditions
- Attest all certificates from the Ministry of Education, educational authorities or councils in the country of study.
- Attest the last certificate that the student passed from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the UAE Embassy or UAE Education and Technology Science Attache (previously known as Cultural Attache) in the country of study.
- Incomplete statements or certificates during the academic year are not valid.
